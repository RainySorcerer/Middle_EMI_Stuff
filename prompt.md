Problem: Middle-class Indians juggle multiple financial products (SIP, credit cards, savings accounts, EMIs) but lack a cohesive system to optimize them. Most tools are either too generic or too expensive.
Why it fits you:

You’ve already explored HDFC credit card mechanics and EMI calculations
You understand SIP investing and financial planning (relevant to your coursework)
High real-world impact in India—your target market understands the problem

What the agent does:

User inputs their financial data securely: monthly income, expenses, credit cards, SIPs, EMIs
AI agent analyzes: “Are you optimizing credit utilization?” “Is this EMI expensive?” “Better SIP allocation?”
Claude generates a monthly financial report with actionable advice
Database stores historical data to track progress
Web UI shows dashboard: current credit utilization, SIP performance, EMI timeline, recommended actions
Example: “Switch ₹5k from Emergency SIP to Growth Fund; use Cashback Card for utilities”

Sample features:

Credit card category matcher (find best card for your spending pattern)
EMI affordability calculator for loans
SIP vs. Lump Sum comparison for goals
Annual tax-saving strategy (80C, 80D awareness)
Expense leak detector (where are you overspending?)
